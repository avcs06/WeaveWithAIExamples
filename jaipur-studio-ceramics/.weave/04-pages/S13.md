## S13 Pieces (/studio/pieces)
Let the owner see all pieces and which glaze-and-size variants are nearly gone or sold out, to plan the next batch.
Roles: shopOwner · Frame: FR02 · Flows: F19, F21

1. Reads the title and knows they are managing pieces and stock.
2. Can choose "Add a piece", the main action, which goes to /studio/pieces/new.
3. Fills in Search pieces (FD25), which shows only pieces whose name or glaze matches the words, keeping the same low-stock and sold-out warnings.
4. Sees each piece as a table row with photo, name (FD01), price (FD03) and stock per glaze-and-size variant, marking variants with 3 or fewer left as low stock and 0 as sold out.
5. Can open a piece by its name or its row, which goes to /studio/pieces/[pieceId].

When no pieces exist yet:
   1. Reads "No pieces yet. Add the first piece to start selling."
   2. Can choose "Add a piece", which goes to /studio/pieces/new.

When no pieces match the search words:
   1. Reads "Nothing matches your search."
   2. Can choose "Clear search", which shows all pieces.
   3. Can choose "Add a piece", which goes to /studio/pieces/new.

Route folder: `web/app/(routes)/(app)/studio/pieces`.
Loads its records through: listPieces.
