## S15 Add a piece (/studio/pieces/new)
Put a new ceramic piece on sale with photos, price, glaze and size variants and stock for each variant.
Roles: shopOwner · Frame: FR02 · Flows: F21

1. Reads the title and knows they are adding a new piece.
2. Fills in the piece form FM01 with Name (FD01), Description (FD02), Price (FD03), Photos (FD04), Category (FD05), Glaze (FD06), Size (FD07) and Variant stock quantity (FD08) for each variant.
3. Can choose "Save", the main action, which saves the piece, shows a toast confirming the piece is saved, and goes to /studio/pieces.
4. Can choose Cancel, which goes to /studio/pieces and changes nothing.

When a required value is missing, the price is not at least ₹1, the variant stock is negative, or a photo is not a valid address:
   1. Sees what is wrong marked and what was typed kept.
   2. Reads the fixes needed for required values, price of at least ₹1, variant stock not negative, or a photo that is not a valid address.

When saving the piece fails:
   1. Sees the entered values kept.
   2. Can choose "Save", which tries saving the piece again.

When the owner cancels without saving:
   1. Reads that nothing was saved.
   2. Sees the piece list as it was at /studio/pieces.

Route folder: `web/app/(routes)/(app)/studio/pieces/new`.
Loads its records through: nothing.
