## S11 Orders (/orders)
Let the owner find orders by fulfilment or payment status, newest first, with new orders clearly identifiable.
Roles: shopOwner · Frame: FR02 · Flows: F13

1. Reads the title and knows they are looking at shop orders.
2. Chooses Order status filter (FD26), which shows only orders matching the chosen fulfilment or payment state, still newest first.
3. Sees each order as a table row with order reference, order date, buyer name, total, payment status and fulfilment status, with New orders clearly marked as new.
4. Can open an order by its reference or its row, which goes to /orders/[orderId].

When there are no orders yet:
   1. Reads "No orders yet. New guest orders will appear here."

When no orders match the chosen filter:
   1. Reads "No orders match the filter."
   2. Can choose "Clear filter", which shows all orders.

Route folder: `web/app/(routes)/(app)/orders`.
Loads its records through: listOrders.
