## S06 The order's reference, as in "Order JSC-1024" (/order-confirmation/[orderId])
Confirm what was ordered, the totals and fulfilment choice, and that the owner will collect payment by UPI later.
Roles: buyer · Frame: FR01 · Flows: F07, F08

1. Reads "Order placed" and the order reference (FD24), as in "Order JSC-1024".
2. Sees the pieces with glaze, size and quantities ordered.
3. Sees the discount and shipping totals and the total.
4. Sees the fulfilment choice, with studio pickup instructions for pickup orders or the courier delivery address for courier orders.
5. Reads "The studio will collect payment by UPI later. No online payment has been taken."
6. Can choose "Find your order later", which goes to /order-lookup.
7. Can choose "Browse pieces", which goes to /pieces.

When the order cannot be found:
   1. Reads "We could not find that order."
   2. Can choose "Find your order", which goes to /order-lookup.
   3. Can choose "Browse pieces", which goes to /pieces.

Route folder: `web/app/(routes)/(marketing)/order-confirmation/[orderId]`.
Loads its records through: getOrder, getPiece.
