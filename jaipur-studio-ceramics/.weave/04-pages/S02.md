## S02 Browse pieces (/pieces)
Let buyers find in-stock mugs, bowls, planters and vases by search words and category, with honest availability.
Roles: buyer · Frame: FR01 · Flows: F02

1. Reads the title and knows they are browsing all pieces.
2. Fills in Search pieces (FD25) and chooses Category (FD05), which shows only pieces matching the search words and category.
3. Sees each piece as a card with its photo, name (FD01), category (FD05), price (FD03) and availability as how many are left or sold out.
4. Can open a piece by its name or its card, which goes to /pieces/[pieceId].

When no pieces are for sale yet:
   1. Reads "No pieces are available right now. The studio is preparing the next batch."
   2. Can choose "Back to the shop", which goes to /.

When no pieces match the search words and category:
   1. Reads "Nothing matches your search."
   2. Sees the typed search words (FD25) and chosen category (FD05) kept as entered.
   3. Can choose "Clear search and category", which shows all pieces again.

When showing the filtered list fails:
   1. Sees the typed search words (FD25) and chosen category (FD05) kept as entered.
   2. Can choose "Try again", which shows the filtered pieces again.

Route folder: `web/app/(routes)/(marketing)/pieces`.
Loads its records through: listPieces.
