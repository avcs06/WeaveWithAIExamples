## S10 Dashboard (/dashboard)
Show the owner what needs packing and what is running low, with sales totals, new orders and low-stock warnings.
Roles: shopOwner · Frame: FR02 · Flows: F12

1. Reads the title and knows this is today's shop summary.
2. Sees the number of New orders, with its label.
3. Sees the total sales amount, with its label.
4. Sees the number of variants with low stock at 3 or fewer left or sold out at 0, with its label.
5. Sees new orders newest first as rows, each with order reference, order date, buyer name, total, payment status and fulfilment status, with New orders clearly marked as new.
6. Can open an order, which goes to /orders/[orderId].
7. Can open Orders, which goes to /orders.
8. Sees pieces with low-stock warnings as rows, each with piece name, glaze, size and stock left, marking 3 or fewer left as low stock and 0 as sold out.
9. Can open a piece, which goes to /studio/pieces/[pieceId].
10. Can open Pieces, which goes to /studio/pieces.

When there are no orders and no pieces yet:
   1. Reads "No orders yet and no pieces yet."
   2. Can choose "Add a piece", which goes to /studio/pieces/new.

When there are pieces but no orders yet:
   1. Sees the counts for new orders and low-stock variants.
   2. Reads "No new orders. New guest orders will appear here."

When there are orders but nothing is low on stock:
   1. Sees the counts for new orders and low-stock variants.
   2. Reads "Nothing is low on stock."

Route folder: `web/app/(routes)/(app)/dashboard`.
Loads its records through: listOrders, listPieces.
