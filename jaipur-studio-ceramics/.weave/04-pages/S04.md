## S04 Your cart (/cart)
Let the buyer check the cart, fix quantities or remove lines, and continue to checkout.
Roles: buyer · Frame: FR01 · Flows: F05

1. Reads the title and knows they are reviewing the cart before checkout.
2. Sees each cart line as a row with piece name, glaze, size, price and quantity (FD09).
3. Sees the subtotal and the shipping note: courier is ₹99, free when the discounted subtotal is more than ₹1,500, and studio pickup is free.
4. Can change a line's quantity (FD09) in place, which updates the cart, recalculates the subtotal and shipping, and shows a toast confirming the cart contents.
5. Can remove a line, which removes it, recalculates the subtotal and shipping, and shows a toast confirming the cart contents.
6. Can choose "Go to checkout", the main action, which goes to /checkout.
7. Can choose "Browse pieces", which goes to /pieces.

When the cart is empty:
   1. Reads "Your cart is empty. Browse pieces to start your order."
   2. Can choose "Browse pieces", which goes to /pieces.

When a quantity is empty, zero, or greater than the stock left for that variant:
   1. Sees the quantity (FD09) marked and what was typed kept.
   2. Reads how many are left for that variant and "Choose a quantity within stock."

When saving the cart change fails:
   1. Sees the cart values kept.
   2. Can choose "Try again", which saves the cart change again.

When a cart line no longer has enough stock left before checkout:
   1. Reads which piece with glaze and size is short and how many are left.
   2. Can change the quantity (FD09) before checkout.

Route folder: `web/app/(routes)/(marketing)/cart`.
Loads its records through: listPieces.
