## S19 Account (/account)
Show the owner's sign-in email and let the owner update the email and password that keep the shop account working.
Roles: shopOwner · Frame: FR02 · Flows: F27

1. Reads the title and knows this is their owner account.
2. Sees the sign-in email (FD22).
3. Can choose "Edit", which shows the editing controls.

When editing::
   1. Fills in Email (FD22) and Password (FD23) with the owner auth form FM05.
   2. Can choose "Done", which saves the new email and password, shows a toast confirming the account is saved, and shows the account with the updated email.

When the email is not an address or the password is shorter than 8 characters while editing:
   1. Sees what is wrong marked and what was typed kept while editing.
   2. Reads the fixes needed for an email that is not an address or a password shorter than 8 characters.

When saving the account changes fails while editing:
   1. Sees the entered values kept while editing.
   2. Can choose "Done", which tries saving the account changes again.

Route folder: `web/app/(routes)/(app)/account`.
Loads its records through: nothing.
