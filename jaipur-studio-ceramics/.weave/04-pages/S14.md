## S14 The piece's name (/studio/pieces/[pieceId])
Let the owner look at one piece and its variant stock before editing or removing it.
Roles: shopOwner · Frame: FR02 · Flows: F20, F22, F23

1. Sees the piece's name (FD01) as the title.
2. Sees the piece's photos (FD04), description (FD02), price (FD03), category (FD05) and each glaze-and-size variant with its stock quantity (FD08), marking low stock at 3 or fewer left and sold out at 0.
3. Can choose "Edit piece", the main action, which goes to /studio/pieces/[pieceId]/edit.
4. Can choose "Remove piece", which opens D01.
5. Can choose "Pieces", which goes to /studio/pieces.

When the piece was removed in the meantime:
   1. Reads "This piece is gone."
   2. Can choose "Pieces", which goes to /studio/pieces.

Route folder: `web/app/(routes)/(app)/studio/pieces/[pieceId]`.
Loads its records through: getPiece.

## D01 Remove this piece? (dialog)
Confirm removing a piece from the shop without deleting its existing orders.
Opened from: S14 · Flows: F23

1. Reads "Remove this piece?" and that existing orders will keep their item snapshot.
2. Can choose Delete, which removes the piece and goes back to /studio/pieces.
3. Can choose Cancel, which closes the dialog and changes nothing.

When the piece is in a New or Packed order:
   1. Reads that the piece cannot be removed while it is in a New or Packed order.
   2. Can choose Cancel, which closes the dialog and changes nothing.

When the piece was already removed:
   1. Reads that the piece is already gone.
   2. Can choose Cancel, which closes the dialog and changes nothing.
