## S16 Edit the piece's name, as in "Edit Speckled Stoneware Mug" (/studio/pieces/[pieceId]/edit)
Update a piece, its price or its variant stock while existing orders keep their item snapshot.
Roles: shopOwner · Frame: FR02 · Flows: F22

1. Reads the title with the piece's name, as in "Edit Speckled Stoneware Mug".
2. Sees the piece form FM01 filled with the current Name (FD01), Description (FD02), Price (FD03), Photos (FD04), Category (FD05), Glaze (FD06), Size (FD07) and Variant stock quantity (FD08).
3. Fills in the changed piece details.
4. Can choose "Save", the main action, which saves the changes, shows a toast confirming the piece is saved, and goes to /studio/pieces/[pieceId].
5. Can choose Cancel, which goes to /studio/pieces/[pieceId] and changes nothing.

When the piece was removed in the meantime:
   1. Reads "This piece is gone."
   2. Can choose "Pieces", which goes to /studio/pieces.

When a required value is missing, the price is not at least ₹1, the variant stock is negative, or a photo is not a valid address:
   1. Sees what is wrong marked and what was typed kept.
   2. Reads the fixes needed for required values, price of at least ₹1, variant stock not negative, or a photo that is not a valid address.

When saving the changes fails:
   1. Sees the entered values kept.
   2. Can choose "Save", which tries saving the changes again.

When the owner cancels without saving:
   1. Reads that nothing was changed.
   2. Sees the piece as it was at /studio/pieces/[pieceId].

Route folder: `web/app/(routes)/(app)/studio/pieces/[pieceId]/edit`.
Loads its records through: getPiece.
