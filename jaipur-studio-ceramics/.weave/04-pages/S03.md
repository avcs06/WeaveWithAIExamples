## S03 The piece's name (/pieces/[pieceId])
Show one piece in detail and let the buyer choose an available glaze, size and quantity and add it to the cart.
Roles: buyer · Frame: FR01 · Flows: F03, F04

1. Sees the piece's name (FD01) as the title.
2. Sees the piece's photos (FD04), description (FD02), price (FD03) and per-variant availability as how many are left or sold out.
3. Chooses Glaze (FD06), Size (FD07) and Quantity (FD09).
4. Sees the availability for that glaze-and-size variant, whether the quantity can be ordered, and the line total.
5. Can choose "Add to cart", the main action, which adds the chosen variant and quantity to the guest cart, stays on /pieces/[pieceId] with a toast confirming what was added with glaze, size, quantity and subtotal, and can open the cart at /cart.
6. Can choose "Browse pieces", which goes to /pieces.

When the piece was removed in the meantime:
   1. Reads "This piece is no longer available."
   2. Can choose "Browse pieces", which goes to /pieces.

When the quantity is empty, zero or not a whole number:
   1. Sees the quantity (FD09) marked, with the chosen glaze (FD06) and size (FD07) kept.
   2. Reads "Choose a quantity of 1 or more."

When checking the variant availability fails:
   1. Sees the chosen glaze (FD06), size (FD07) and quantity (FD09) kept.
   2. Can choose "Try again", which checks the variant availability again.

When the quantity is more than the stock left for the chosen glaze-and-size variant:
   1. Reads how many are left for that glaze-and-size variant.
   2. Can lower the quantity (FD09) to the stock left.

When the chosen glaze-and-size variant is sold out:
   1. Reads "That glaze and size is sold out and cannot be ordered."
   2. Can choose another glaze (FD06) or size (FD07).

When adding to the cart fails:
   1. Sees the chosen glaze (FD06), size (FD07) and quantity (FD09) kept.
   2. Can choose "Add to cart", which tries adding the chosen variant again.

When the stock left changed and the quantity can no longer be added:
   1. Reads how many are left for that glaze-and-size variant.
   2. Can change the quantity (FD09) to the stock left.

Route folder: `web/app/(routes)/(marketing)/pieces/[pieceId]`.
Loads its records through: getPiece.
