## S01 Jaipur Studio Ceramics (/)
Introduce the Jaipur studio's own ceramics shop, show featured in-stock pieces and start buyers browsing without messaging the studio.
Roles: buyer · Frame: FR01 · Flows: F01

1. Reads the headline "Handmade ceramics from our Jaipur studio".
2. Reads "Small-batch mugs, bowls, planters and vases, made in Jaipur and sold only while they are in stock. Order here, no messaging needed."
3. Can choose "Browse pieces", the main action, which goes to /pieces.
4. Sees a preview of featured in-stock pieces as cards, with a piece called "Speckled Stoneware Mug" at ₹650 with 8 left, a bowl at ₹850 with 5 left and a planter at ₹1,200 with 3 left, each with a photo, price and how many are left.
5. Can open a featured piece, which goes to /pieces/[pieceId].
6. Reads "Made in small batches" and "Each glaze and size shows the real stock left, so what you order is what we have."
7. Reads "Order without messaging" and "Add pieces to your cart and place your order here in a few calm steps."
8. Reads "Pay by UPI later" and "No online payment is taken. The studio will collect payment by UPI after you order."
9. Reads "Pickup or courier" and "Pick up at the Jaipur studio for free, or choose courier delivery for ₹99, free on discounted orders of more than ₹1,500."

When no pieces are in stock to feature:
   1. Reads "Everything is sold out and a new batch is being made."
   2. Can choose "Browse pieces", which goes to /pieces.

Route folder: `web/app/(routes)/(marketing)`.
Loads its records through: listPieces.
